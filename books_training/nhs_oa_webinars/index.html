NHS-OA Community: Webinar Recordings


Summary

A YouTube playlist of recorded webinars from the NHS-OA Community and its predecessor, the NHS-R Community. Mostly focused on R, the webinars cover tools and techniques such as Shiny, Quarto, Git, package development, testing and reproducible environments, alongside real-world NHS case studies in forecasting, statistical modelling and service planning.

Contents

  • Search the webinars

This YouTube playlist collects recordings of webinars run by the NHS-OA Community, including those run when it was the NHS-R Community. Each webinar is a standalone session given by analysts, data scientists and other speakers from the NHS and beyond.

The webinars are mostly focused on R, and include:

  • tools and good practice - such as Shiny, Quarto, Git and GitHub, writing R packages, unit testing, package management and reproducible environments with renv
  • analytical techniques - such as time series forecasting, causal inference, statistical process control and statistical disclosure control
  • NHS case studies - such as predicting emergency demand for beds, assessing the impact of COVID-19 on hip replacement rates, and modelling the consequences of emergency department admission delays
