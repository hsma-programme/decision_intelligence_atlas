HSMA: Project Showcase Recordings


Summary

Recordings of project presentations from the showcases of the Health Service Modelling Associates (HSMA) programme, where participants present the modelling and data science projects they have carried out in their own health, social care and policing organisations. Projects use techniques such as discrete event simulation, machine learning, natural language processing, forecasting and geographic modelling.

In the second phase of the Health Service Modelling Associates (HSMA) programme, participants carry out a modelling or data science project for their own health, social care or policing organisation. At the end of each round, they present their projects at a showcase, and the recordings are published on the HSMA YouTube channel.

The projects tackle real-world problems using the techniques taught on the programme, such as:

  • discrete event simulation of pathways, from emergency departments and urgent treatment centres to mental health and neurodevelopmental services
  • machine learning and natural language processing, for example predicting admissions or non-attendance and analysing free text
  • forecasting demand, from A&E attendances to excess mortality
  • geographic modelling, for example of travel times and ambulance dispatch boundaries

Some showcases also include talks from HSMA alumni about what they have done since the programme. During HSMA 5, the PenCHORD team who run HSMA also presented a series of showcases of their own projects, as inspiration for participants’ project ideas. For HSMA 3, there is also a recording of the whole final presentation event.

More details about many of the projects, including links to their code where it is shared, can be found on the HSMA website.
