NHS-OA Community: NHS-R Podcast


Summary

The NHS-R Community’s podcast, first released on SoundCloud from 2022 and now on YouTube with edited subtitles. Its episodes cover the history of R and the NHS-R Community, learning R, the team behind the COVID-19 dashboard, and a text mining project for Friends and Family Test feedback.

Collection of podcast episodes with many different guests, so it is not ranked as a whole.

  • Episodes

The NHS-R Podcast was made by the NHS-R Community, now the NHS-OA Community, and first released on SoundCloud from 2022. The episodes have since been uploaded to YouTube, with automatic subtitles that have been checked and edited by hand.

The episodes feature people from across the community talking about their work and experiences:

  • Episode 0 - a pilot episode in which Chris Beeley and Tom Jemmett talk about R, its uses, and their experiences of learning it
  • Episode 1 - the history of R, the NHS-R Community and its conferences, with Professor Mohammed Mohammed, Paul Stroner, Sarah Culkin, Jenny Morgan, Ellen Coughlan and Chris Beeley (in two parts)
  • Episode 2 - Chris Beeley talks to the Public Health England team behind the COVID-19 dashboard
  • Episode 3 - Chris Beeley talks to Andreas Soteriades about a text mining project for Friends and Family Test feedback
